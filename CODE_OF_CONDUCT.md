# Code of conduct

Lettuce is built for a welcoming, safe Roblox development community.

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, scam, or discriminate against anyone.
- Do not use Lettuce to transfer content you do not have permission to use.
- Adult content and any behavior that endangers minors are prohibited.
- Report security problems privately according to `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these rules. Serious safety concerns should also be reported to the appropriate platform or authorities.
